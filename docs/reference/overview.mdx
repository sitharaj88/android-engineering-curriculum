## How to use this section

<Callout type="tip">
These pages are designed for **scan-reading**, not start-to-finish. Use
`Cmd/Ctrl + F` to jump straight to what you need.
</Callout>

- **Preparing for interviews?** Work the [Question Bank](/docs/reference/interview-question-bank)
  cover-to-cover; expect each question to take 3-5 minutes of out-loud
  practice.
- **Stuck on a syntax?** The [Cheat Sheets](/docs/reference/cheat-sheets)
  are the fastest path to the right incantation.
- **Don't know what a term means?** [Glossary](/docs/reference/glossary)
  has one-line definitions plus links to the chapter that teaches it.
- **Modernizing a legacy codebase?** [Migration Playbooks](/docs/reference/migration-playbooks)
  walk you through the safe sequence — and the gotchas — for each common
  migration.

## Living documents

These pages evolve faster than the curriculum chapters. When a new
Compose API ships or a deprecation lands, the cheat sheets get patched
first. Treat them as the most current source of truth.
