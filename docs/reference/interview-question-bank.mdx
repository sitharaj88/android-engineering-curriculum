# Interview Question Bank

100+ questions Android engineers actually face at L4–L6 interviews,
grouped by category. Treat as drill material — for each, prepare a
30-second answer + a 2-minute deep dive.

<Callout kind="tip" title="How to use this">
Don't memorize answers. Practice **explaining the underlying concept**
in your own words. Use a peer or rubber duck. If you can't explain
something simply, you don't understand it yet — go re-read the relevant
chapter.
</Callout>

## How to use this bank

<Callout kind="success" title="Drill plan">
**Week 1**: Pick 10 random questions per category. Answer aloud. Time each (target 30s for fact-based, 2-3 min for system design / behavioral).

**Week 2**: Record yourself answering 5 system design and 5 behavioral. Re-listen. Note ums, tangents, missing structure.

**Week 3**: Do 3 mock interviews with peers — one technical, one system design, one behavioral.

**Week 4**: Tighten the weakest 10 questions. Refresh story bank.
</Callout>
